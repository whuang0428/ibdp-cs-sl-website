# Phishing and Spear Phishing

## 1. Lesson Goals

By the end of this lesson, students should be able to:

- define phishing and spear phishing
- identify common warning signs in suspicious messages
- explain how phishing threatens accounts and data
- distinguish mass phishing and targeted phishing
- apply safe verification and reporting steps
- write exam-style answers about phishing prevention

## 3. Key Terms

| English Term | 中文解释 | Exam-style meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Phishing | 网络钓鱼 | A fraudulent message that tricks users into revealing information or taking unsafe actions |
| Spear phishing | 定向钓鱼 | A targeted phishing attack customized for a specific person or organization |
| Credential theft | 凭据盗取 | Stealing usernames, passwords, or verification codes |
| Suspicious link | 可疑链接 | A link that may lead to an unsafe or fake website |
| Attachment | 附件 | A file included with a message |
| Sender spoofing | 发件人伪装 | Making a message appear to come from someone else |
| Reporting | 报告 | Sending suspicious activity to the correct support or security contact |

### English Explanation

**Phishing** uses email, messages, QR codes, social media, or other channels to trick users into clicking links, entering passwords, downloading attachments, or making payments.

**Spear phishing** is more targeted. The attacker may use information about the target's school, course, teacher, classmates, or events to make the message look more believable.

Common warning signs:

- unusual sender address
- unusual link domain
- request for password, verification code, or payment
- urgency or fear
- unexpected attachment
- message does not match normal procedure
- strange greeting or writing style

Safe response: do not click, do not enter credentials, verify through official channels, and report.

## 5. Real-life Example

### Example: Fake LMS Login

A student receives:

> Your assignment grade is ready. Log in here to view it.

The link leads to a domain that looks similar to the school LMS but is not the official domain.

This could be phishing because it tries to capture login credentials.

---

## 6. Defensive Recognition Pattern

```text
Unexpected message
→ check sender and link
→ identify request type
→ do not enter credentials
→ open official site manually if needed
→ report suspicious message
```

---

## 7. Scenario Analysis

### Scenario

A message says:

> Dear student, your scholarship payment is blocked. Open the attached form and enter your school login.

| Warning Sign | Explanation |
|---|---|
| unexpected money-related message | unusual context |
| attachment | may contain malicious content |
| asks for school login | credential theft risk |
| pressure | payment is “blocked” |

---

## 8. Explanation of the Scenario

The message may be phishing. It combines financial pressure with a credential request. The student should not open the attachment and should verify with the official scholarship or school office using known contact details.

---

## 9. Step-by-step Defensive Response

| Step | Defensive Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Do not click links or open attachments immediately |
| 2 | Check sender address and domain |
| 3 | Hover or inspect link safely without opening |
| 4 | Visit official website manually if necessary |
| 5 | Never enter passwords from message links |
| 6 | Report suspicious message |
| 7 | If credentials were entered, change password and notify support |

---

## 10. Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why it is a problem | Better habit |
|---|---|---|
| Clicking to check if link is real | This may expose the user to risk | Use official website manually |
| Trusting display name only | Display names can be spoofed | Check full sender address |
| Assuming targeted details mean safe | Spear phishing uses personal details | Still verify |
| Opening unexpected attachments | Attachments may be unsafe | Confirm with sender through trusted channel |
| Feeling embarrassed after clicking | Delay increases risk | Report quickly |

## 11. Guided Practice

### Practice 1

What is the difference between phishing and spear phishing?

<details><summary>Suggested Answer</summary>

Phishing is often broad and sent to many people. Spear phishing is targeted and customized for a specific person or organization.

</details>

### Practice 2

Give two warning signs of phishing.

<details><summary>Suggested Answer</summary>

A suspicious link domain and a request for password or verification code.

</details>

## 13. Exam-style / Reflection Questions

### Question 1 [2 marks]

State what is meant by phishing.

<details><summary>Mark Scheme Style Answer</summary>

Phishing is a fraudulent message designed to trick users into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions, such as entering passwords or clicking malicious links.

</details>

### Question 2 [4 marks]

Explain why spear phishing may be more dangerous than general phishing.

<details><summary>Mark Scheme Style Answer</summary>

Spear phishing is customized for a specific target or organization. It may include names, courses, job roles, or current events, making it appear more believable. This increases the chance that the target trusts the message and follows the request.

</details>
