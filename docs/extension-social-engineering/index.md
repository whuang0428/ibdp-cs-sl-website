# Social Engineering Awareness

## Unit Overview

This section is a school extension connected to A2 Networks, cybersecurity, and digital ethics.

The purpose is not to teach students how to attack people. The purpose is to help students understand how manipulation-based attacks work at a high level, recognize warning signs, protect themselves and organizations, and respond safely.

## Teaching Principles

1. Keep the topic defensive and ethical.
2. Use safe fictional examples only.
3. Do not ask students to send real phishing messages or impersonate real people.
4. Focus on recognition signs, verification steps, reporting, and prevention.
5. Connect to school life: email, LMS, shared drives, passwords, QR codes, USB devices, visitor access, and online accounts.

::: danger Important Safety Rule
Avoid practical attack role-play against real people, real accounts, real organizations, or real websites. Classroom activities should use fictional messages, printed cards, and controlled examples.
:::
